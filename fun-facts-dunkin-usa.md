# Fun Facts: Dunkin' Locations Across the USA

**The first ever Dunkin' was created in 1950 at the street address 543 Southern Artery, Quincy, MA 02169. It originally opened in 1948 as the "Open Kettle" before rebranding in 1950.**

**There are a total of 10,523 locations across 47 regions in the USA (46 states + Washington D.C.).**

*Distribution is heavily right-skewed. Mean is 224 while median is Alabama at 77.*

**Four states** have **0 locations**: Idaho, Montana, Oregon, and Washington. The Pacific Northwest is the sparsest region in the USA.

**North Dakota** has **1 single** Dunkin' location. The only state that has a single location.

**Two states** have **2 locations**: Alaska and South Dakota.

The **top 5 states** hold **48.4%** of all locations:

1. New York - 1,484 (14.1%)
2. Massachusetts - 1,035 (9.8%)
3. Florida - 960 (9.1%)
4. New Jersey - 871 (8.3%)
5. Illinois - 743 (7.1%)

That's a total of **5,093 locations** in the top 5 states. With the top 2 (NY, MA) summing to **23.9%**.

New England has a total of **2,094 locations** alone, which is **19.9%** of total locations across the country.

New York has more locations alone than the bottom 28 listings (**1,484** to **1,380**).

**90%** of all Dunkin' locations sit east of the Mississippi. Everything west sums to **1,055 stores** (10% of locations).

New Hampshire slightly edges out Massachusetts as the top spot for Dunkin' per 100,000 (population density). NH sits at **15.5 locations per 100,000 people** while MA sits at **14.5 per 100,000**.

Rhode Island is the most geographically dense at **1 Dunkin' per 6.5 square miles** of land.

Alaska is the least geographically dense at **1 Dunkin' per 285,000 square miles** of land.

---

*Sources: Dunkin's official store directory, location counts per state/region as listed in October 2026. Per-capita and per-square-mile figures use recent U.S. Census population estimates and Census land areas, so treat those as approximations.*
